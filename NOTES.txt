DisasterMart — redesigned runnable storefront

Run: extract this package and open index.html in a modern browser.
No build, installation, internet connection, or dependencies are required.
Optional local server: python -m http.server 8000 --directory disastermart-redesign

Features: responsive storefront, search, categories, price/name sorting,
product detail dialogs, editable cart, guest checkout with inline validation,
itemized cost summary, preserved form entries when returning to the cart,
and simulated order confirmation.

Important: this is a fictional demonstration, not a production commerce system.
No payments, shipments, subscriptions, or real orders are processed.
Use invented contact/address details only. No data is sent to a server.
Cart and form values stay in page memory; refresh clears them.
Product specifications absent from the original demonstration are not invented.
Taxes are not calculated. Demo shipping is $7.13 per nonempty order.

For production, implement a secure commerce backend, actual inventory and
product content, shipping and tax services, genuine policies, payment provider,
server-side price/quantity validation, and order confirmations.
