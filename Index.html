UI/UX Mistakes Demonstrated

  • Clashing colors and unreadable contrast
  • Misaligned cards and inconsistent spacing
  • Unclear navigation and hidden search/cart
  • Conflicting typography and visual hierarchy
  • Vague form labels and distant errors
  • Unnecessary fields and checkout steps
  • Intrusive promotions and fake loading
  • Inconsistent buttons and quantity labels
  • Tiny text and unhelpful image descriptions
  • Repeated ads competing with essential content

For discussion: these choices increase cognitive load, obscure important actions, and exclude users. Moving elements use slow animation, not rapid flashing. Motion and pop-ups can be disabled above. Keyboard access remains usable for a safe demonstration.
